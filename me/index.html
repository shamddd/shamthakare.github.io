Systems Architecture & Software Engineering Leadership:

My work in systems engineering addresses the twin imperatives of modern cloud architecture: hardware-conscious low-latency software design and provably resilient distributed fault tolerance:

1. High-Throughput C++20 Consensus Engine (quorumshift)

Engineered a dynamic quorum re-weighting Raft consensus engine in modern C++20 using lock-free concurrency and asynchronous coroutines. Eliminates p99 tail latency degradation under asymmetric network partitioning while maintaining strict state-machine linearizability.

2. High-Assurance Runtime Protection Platforms (medirush-safeagent)

Formulated multi-stage deterministic runtime policy interception and out-of-band schema validation barriers that decouple untrusted autonomous actions from system state mutations, achieving zero unhandled runtime side-effects in critical execution environments.

3. Civic & Engineering Leadership (Social Volunteer Club)

Served as elected President of the university Social Volunteer Club (2023–2024), mobilizing and managing 250+ volunteers across regional public health and educational initiatives that directly impacted 1,200+ individuals across Maharashtra.
