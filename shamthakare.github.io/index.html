About Me

I am an independent computer science researcher specializing in the design, safety, and performance of adaptive autonomous systems. My research spans four primary pillars: diagnosing representation geometry and plasticity in reinforcement learning checkpoints prior to post-training alignment, engineering zero-knowledge remote attestation and dynamic access-frequency-weighted Oblivious RAM for confidential hardware enclaves, developing failure-aware dynamic quorum adaptation algorithms for fault-tolerant distributed consensus, and constructing graph-constrained topological causal reasoning engines over multi-modal cloud telemetry.
