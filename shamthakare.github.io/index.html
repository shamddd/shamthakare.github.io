Research Statement

I conduct foundational and systems research on Trustworthy, Adaptive, and Verifiable Autonomous Systems. My work investigates performance, safety, and verifiability guarantees for autonomous software operating in dynamic, non-stationary environments—spanning reinforcement learning reasoning diagnostics, agent state failure recovery, failure-aware distributed consensus, and zero-knowledge telemetry verification.

Currently based in Pune, India, I work as a Data Engineer / ML Specialist at Spectra Corporate Service while maintaining an independent research program. I am preparing applications for Computer Science PhD programs.

When Confidence Proxies Confound Reasoning Complexity: Pitfalls of Uncertainty-Weighted Credit Assignment in LM RL

Sham Satish Thakare

Primary Finding: Falsifies sample-level uncertainty-weighted GRPO credit assignment on multi-step reasoning. Demonstrates that token predictive entropy ($r = +0.486$), mean NLL ($r = +0.432$), and logit margin ($r = +0.495$) track derivation length, misidentifying correct complex derivations as uncertain in 42.1% of cases. Sample-level consensus weighting (CA-GRPO) yields 0.00% Pass@1 gain over standard outcome-supervised GRPO across 3 matched seeds.

Amortized Intervention Frontiers for Language-Model Reasoning: When Does Training Beat Search?

Sham Satish Thakare
