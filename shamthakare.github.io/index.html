recovery_eval: State-Matched and Provenance-Aware Evaluation of Recovery Behavior in Language-Model Reasoning

Sham Satish Thakare

Introduces a reproducible data-centric evaluation framework that compares verifier-defined recovery states with prospectively matched reference-control states using frozen structural covariates. Evaluated on 400 genuine neural continuations from Qwen2.5-Math 1.5B Base and Instruct checkpoints across 20 GSM8K problems. Measured a matched recovery-specific contrast of -0.110 (95% bootstrap CI: [-0.240, +0.030]). Under the state-matched protocol, we did not observe evidence of a recovery-specific advantage for the Instruct checkpoint over the Base checkpoint.
