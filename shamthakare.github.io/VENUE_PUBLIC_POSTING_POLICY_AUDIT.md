## Policy Compliance Summary

| Category | Policy Rule | Compliance Status | Action Taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preprint Posting** | IEEE allows authors to post preprints of submitted papers on their personal websites, institutional repositories, and arXiv. | **PASSED** | Article is posted on author's personal academic site ([`shamthakare.github.io`](https://shamddd.github.io/shamthakare.github.io/)). |
| **IEEE Copyright Notice** | Preprints of submitted papers should state: *"This work has been submitted to the IEEE for possible publication."* | **PASSED** | Added standard IEEE submission disclosure to paper reference block and PDF links. |
| **Manuscript ID Removal** | Internal ScholarOne tracking IDs (`TAI-2026-Aug-A-01878`) should NOT be displayed publicly as credentials. | **PASSED** | Removed all administrative manuscript IDs from public HTML headers, badges, titles, SEO tags, and OpenGraph metadata. |
| **Working Paper Labeling** | For manuscripts pending resubmission upload, public status should use `Working Paper, 2026` or `Submitted Manuscript`. | **PASSED** | Public article header displays: **August 2026 · Working Paper / Research Note**. |
| **Double-Blind Integrity** | Public research blogging on personal websites does not violate IEEE TAI submission rules, provided manuscript IDs and administrative metadata are not publicly associated with formal peer review decisions. | **PASSED** | Kept administrative review files private and strictly un-indexed. |
