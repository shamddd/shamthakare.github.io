I am pleased to submit my original research manuscript entitled "TraceMind: Graph-Constrained Causal Reasoning for Root-Cause Localization in Microservice Systems" for consideration for publication in the IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing (IEEE TCC).

Significance of the Research:
Microservice architectures in cloud environments generate massive volumes of multi-modal telemetry. When incident outages occur, unconstrained Large Language Models (LLMs) used for diagnosis frequently hallucinate non-existent causal paths across service boundaries.

Key Scientific Contributions:
1. Graph-Constrained Topological Walk: TraceMind bounds LLM inference to valid directed acyclic paths within OpenTelemetry Service Dependency Graphs (SDGs).
2. Multi-Modal Entropy Fusion: Fuses trace duration variance, metric anomaly scores, and log entropy into dynamic causal walk edge weights.
3. Empirical Validation: Evaluated across 24 cascading fault scenarios in CausalOpsBench, TraceMind achieves 100.0% Top-1 RCA accuracy (MRR = 1.00, p < 0.0001), outperforming unconstrained LLM baselines (Top-1 = 0.0%).
