2. Topological Invariants & Quorum Overlap in Dynamic Hypergraphs

In asynchronous distributed consensus, state machine linearizability reduces to establishing an invariant non-empty intersection between any two dynamically formed quorums: $\mathcal{Q}_i \cap \mathcal{Q}_j \neq \emptyset$. By modeling dynamic quorum allocation as a hypergraph coloring problem over time-varying communication graphs under bounded clock drift $\Delta$, I derived analytical proofs showing that dynamic re-weighting strictly preserves the majority intersection property under arbitrary asymmetric link degradation.

3. Counterfactual Divergence Metric Formulations

Formulated state-contrast continuation divergence: $$D_{\text{recovery}} = \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{D}} [\Delta_{\text{recovery}}(x)] - \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{D}} [\Delta_{\text{control}}(x)]$$ establishing difference-in-differences estimators that isolate genuine self-correcting trajectory transitions from unconditional generative capacity under frozen structural covariates.
