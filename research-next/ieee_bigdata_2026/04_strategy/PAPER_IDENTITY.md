## 1. CANONICAL PAPER METADATA

* **Working Title**: *"A State-Matched Framework for Evaluating Recovery Behavior in Language-Model Reasoning"*
* **Primary Contribution**: A reproducible state-matched evaluation framework for measuring recovery behavior in language-model reasoning by comparing verifier-defined recovery states with structurally matched controls while maintaining primitive rollout provenance and explicit evaluation-exposure tracking.
* **Research Problem**: Current reasoning evaluation conflates state difficulty with general continuation quality, making it impossible to isolate whether a post-trained policy specifically improves recovery from intermediate errors or simply excels on easier prefixes.
* **IEEE BigData Relevance**: Benchmarking complex LLM reasoning produces massive, heterogeneous trajectory streams. Our framework provides standardized data-centric AI infrastructure (state matching, exposure ledgers, primitive JSONL provenance) for big data reasoning evaluation pipelines.
* **Explicit Banned Terms**: `causal`, `emergent capability`, `new reasoning strategy`, `structural intelligence`, `first`, `unprecedented`, `uncolonized`.
