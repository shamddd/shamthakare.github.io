When Confidence Proxies Confound Reasoning Complexity: Pitfalls of Uncertainty-Weighted Credit Assignment in Language Model Reinforcement Learning
Sham Satish Thakare
Under Review at IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence (IEEE TAI)

Falsifies token-predictive entropy as an uncertainty proxy in LM RLVR. Demonstrates that predictive entropy correlates strongly with sequence length ($r = +0.486$), causing uncertainty-weighted GRPO to systematically misallocate credit on valid multi-step reasoning derivations.
