Reasoning Reliability & RLVR // REASON

When Confidence Proxies Confound Reasoning Complexity

“Can uncertainty proxies accidentally encode reasoning complexity rather than step invalidity?”

A forensic diagnostic audit exposing sequence-length confounding in token predictive entropy, proving nominal MC-dropout determinism on modern LLMs, and demonstrating that uncertainty-weighted credit assignment produces zero observed online advantage over standard outcome-supervised GRPO.

Code RepositoryDownload Manuscript (PDF)Read Interactive Essay

01. PROBLEM FORMULATION

In reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), researchers frequently attempt to weight policy gradients using token-level predictive entropy or self-consistency scores. The core assumption is that high-entropy tokens correspond to critical decision forks. However, if token entropy is structurally confounded with completion length and linguistic branching factor, policy gradient weighting disproportionately penalizes complex, valid mathematical derivations.

02. METHODOLOGY & CONTROLS

  • 01Constructed PyTorch probing suite evaluating token predictive entropy, mean negative log-likelihood (NLL), and logit margin across untouched GSM8K prompts (N=100) using Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct.
  • 02Computed zero-order Pearson correlations and partial correlations controlling for completion token length.
  • 03Conducted MC-dropout variance audits across 50 forward passes on modern architectures without active dropout layers.
  • 04Formulated a 5-way negative control ablation suite: (1) Standard Outcome GRPO, (2) Entropy-Weighted GRPO, (3) Inverse-Length Weighted GRPO, (4) Uniform Random Perturbation, (5) Shuffled Advantage Control across N=3 random seeds.

FAILURE MODES

  • •Token entropy spikes during benign mathematical formatting tags (e.g. \boxed{} or LaTeX syntax switches).
  • •Confidence-weighted policy updates aggressively down-weight lengthy, rigorous proofs in favor of terse, risky guesses.
  • •Length-unaware reward shaping introduces severe reward hacking toward degenerate short-sequence trajectories.

SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS

  • •Evaluated on Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct; larger 7B/70B model lineages may exhibit different token entropy distributions.
  • •Primary empirical benchmark restricted to arithmetic reasoning on GSM8K; multi-modal reasoning was not examined.
  • •Focuses on post-training policy gradient credit assignment rather than inference-time tree search (MCTS).
