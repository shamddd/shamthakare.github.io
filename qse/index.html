Quantum Science & Engineering Research Vision:

My doctoral research in Quantum Science and Engineering sits at the intersection of mathematical physics, quantum complexity theory, and circuit compilation optimization:

1. Multi-Qubit Density Matrix Mechanics & Entanglement Measures

Formally trained in density operator formalism for mixed quantum states $\rho \in \mathcal{S}(\mathcal{H})$, satisfying $\rho \succeq 0$ and $\text{Tr}(\rho) = 1$. Investigated von Neumann entropy $S(\rho) = -\text{Tr}(\rho \log \rho)$ and quantum fidelity $F(\rho, \sigma) = (\text{Tr}\sqrt{\sqrt{\rho}\sigma\sqrt{\rho}})^2$ to bound distinguishability under trace-preserving completely positive (CPTP) noise maps.

2. Stabilizer Circuits, Fault-Tolerant Syndrome Cycles & Error Mitigation

Conducted peer mentorship and research into stabilizer code projections ($S_i |\psi\rangle = |\psi\rangle$) and the Gottesman-Knill theorem. Modeled fault-tolerant syndrome extraction cycles and zero-noise extrapolation (ZNE) techniques using Qiskit and Cirq, assessing fidelity decay across tensor network contractions (MPS/PEPS) under realistic depolarizing noise models.

3. Quantum Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Separations

Investigating theoretical complexity separations $\text{BPP} \subseteq \text{BQP} \subseteq \text{PSPACE}$, quantum query complexity bounds, and optimal circuit compilation for neutral-atom array topologies with constrained Rydberg interaction geometries.
