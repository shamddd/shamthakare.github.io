**Author**: Sham Satish Thakare  
**Last Audit Date**: August 21, 2026

## Separation Protocol

1. **Blog vs Manuscript Separation**: The research article published on `shamthakare.github.io/writing/` is a research communication and visual explanation artifact. The formal manuscript (`TAI-2026-Aug-A-01878_Main_Manuscript.pdf`) remains the canonical academic publication.
2. **Text Transfer Boundary**: No AI-generated web prose, blog summary text, or informal explanations from the web article shall be copied into the formal scientific manuscript without explicit author review and compliance check against IEEE AI disclosure policies.
3. **Figure Transfer Boundary**: All SVG diagrams generated for the website (`hero-concept.svg`, `zero-dropout-audit.svg`, etc.) are generated programmatically from verified JSON data (`figure-data.json`). If converted to high-resolution PDF for journal resubmission, their data provenance remains linked to `figure-data.json`.
