\noindent\textbf{Note on Multiplicity Adjustment}: Confidence intervals reported in Table~\ref{tab:supp_trajectory} are unadjusted $95\%$ problem-blocked percentile bootstrap intervals. Under family-wise Bonferroni adjustment across the four intermediate trajectory checkpoints ($M=4, \alpha_{\text{Bonf}}=0.0125$), the simultaneous intervals are: $t=32$: $[-0.0100, +0.0789]$ ($p_{\text{raw}}=0.019$); $t=96$: $[+0.0306, +0.1199]$ ($p_{\text{raw}}<0.0001$); $t=160$: $[+0.0149, +0.1022]$ ($p_{\text{raw}}=0.0010$); $t=224$: $[+0.0544, +0.1365]$ ($p_{\text{raw}}<0.0001$). Consequently, while the unadjusted contrast at $t=32$ is positive, it does not achieve statistical significance under conservative family-wise multiplicity control.
