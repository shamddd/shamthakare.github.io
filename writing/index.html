When Confidence Proxies Confound Reasoning Complexity

Question: Can common uncertainty proxies confound sequence completion length with mathematical error during RLVR post-training? A diagnostic audit exposing completion-length confounding ($r = 0.486, N=100$) in token predictive entropy, proving MC-dropout determinism on zero-dropout LLMs, and demonstrating via preregistered negative controls that consistency weighting produced zero observed online advantage ($d = 0.00, N=3$ seeds) over outcome-supervised GRPO.
