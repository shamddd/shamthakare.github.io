Biography & Background

Sham Satish Thakare is a pre-doctoral researcher focusing on reliable reasoning and adaptive computation in foundation models and high-performance distributed consensus. He received his B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science from Savitribai Phule Pune University (APCOER Pune) in June 2024 with a cumulative GPA of 8.70 / 10.00 (Final Year SGPA: 9.53 / 10.00, First Class with Distinction).

His research investigates credit assignment failures in language model reinforcement learning (RLVR), showing that token-predictive entropy correlates strongly with reasoning length and misallocates reward on multi-step derivations. In parallel, he develops difference-in-differences counterfactual evaluation frameworks to measure neural reasoning error recovery across post-training checkpoints.

Since June 2024, Sham conducts pre-doctoral systems research at Capital One under the direct mentorship of Yuri Shelke (Distinguished Engineer). Together, they formulate dynamic quorum adaptation algorithms and failure-domain aware replica selection in C++20 Raft consensus clusters (quorumshift), eliminating tail latency spikes under asymmetric network faults.
