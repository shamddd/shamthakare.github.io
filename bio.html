Bio:

Sham Satish Thakare is an independent computer science researcher and Pre-Doctoral Researcher at Capital One based in Pune, India. His research broadly investigates the scientific and systems principles governing foundation model reasoning reliability, reinforcement learning credit assignment (RLVR), and fault-tolerant distributed consensus protocols.

At Capital One (2024–Present), Sham conducts pre-doctoral systems research under the direct mentorship of Yuri Shelke (Distinguished Engineer at Capital One). Together, they formulated failure-domain aware dynamic quorum adaptation algorithms and latency profiling mechanisms in C++20 Raft consensus clusters (quorumshift), eliminating p99 tail latency degradation under asymmetric network partitions and non-uniform round-trip delays.

In foundation model post-training, Sham's recent work falsifies token-predictive entropy as an uncertainty proxy in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (GRPO), demonstrating substantial completion-length confounding ($r = +0.486$) and developing length-controlled credit allocation estimators. In parallel, he formulated state-matched difference-in-differences counterfactual evaluation protocols (recovery_eval and stateshift) to measure whether language models recover from intermediate reasoning errors under frozen structural covariates.
