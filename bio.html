Biography & Background

Sham Satish Thakare is an independent computer science researcher and machine learning specialist based in Pune, India. He received his B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science from APCOER Pune in June 2024 with a cumulative GPA of 8.70 / 10.00.

His research focuses on reliable reasoning and adaptive computation for foundation models, investigating token entropy length confounding in reinforcement learning credit assignment (RLVR) and state-matched continuation error recovery evaluation.

Since June 2024, Sham works as a Data Engineer / Machine Learning Specialist at Spectra Corporate Service in Pune, India, engineering high-throughput data processing systems and machine learning data pipelines. Previously, he served as a Research Intern at APCOER Pune (2023–2024), conducting research on machine learning algorithms and providing teaching support for undergraduate coursework in algorithms and machine learning.
