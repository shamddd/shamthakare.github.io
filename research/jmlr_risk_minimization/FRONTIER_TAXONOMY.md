# TAXONOMY OF THREE DISTINCT DEPLOYMENT FRONTIERS

1. **$Q^*_{	ext{cost}}$ (Raw Cost Equality)**: The query volume where total FLOPs are identical ($C_{	ext{total}}(A_1, Q) = C_{	ext{total}}(A_3, Q)$), regardless of accuracy differences.
2. **$Q^*_{	ext{utility}}(u)$ (Utility-Matched Crossover)**: The query volume where up-front training compute is amortized under the constraint that both methods satisfy target utility $u$.
3. **$Q^*_{	ext{Pareto}}$ (Pareto-Optimal Set Transition)**: The query volume where intervention $b$ enters the optimal utility-cost Pareto frontier.

**Primary Manuscript Object**: **$Q^*_{	ext{utility}}(u)$**.
