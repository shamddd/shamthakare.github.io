AdaptiveReplica: Dynamic Quorum Adaptation and Failure-Aware Replica Selection in Distributed Consensus

Sham Satish Thakare

Working Paper • Distributed Systems Research (2026)

Abstract

In state-machine replication protocols such as Raft, static quorum commitments suffer from tail-latency degradation under asymmetric network faults, straggling peers, and non-uniform round-trip delays. In this paper, we formulate AdaptiveReplica—a failure-domain aware dynamic quorum adaptation mechanism implemented in C++20 (quorumshift). AdaptiveReplica continuously re-weights consensus quorums without violating linearizability invariants or safety thresholds, eliminating tail latency spikes under asymmetric fault injections.
