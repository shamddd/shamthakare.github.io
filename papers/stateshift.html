Sham Satish Thakare

Working Paper / Technical Report (Historical Submission: Elsevier AIJ ARTINT-D-26-01491)

Abstract

Formulates state-matched continuation evaluation protocols under verifier perturbations on neural reasoning rollouts. Evaluates structural covariate controls and error recovery dynamics across language-model reasoning trajectories.
