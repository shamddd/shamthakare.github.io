## 1. Primary References (Verified)

1. **ToolShield & MT-AgentRisk (2024)**:
   - *Title*: *Unsafer in Many Turns: Benchmarking and Defending Multi-Turn Safety Risks in Tool-Using Agents*
   - *Authors*: Authors of ToolShield & MT-AgentRisk
   - *Venue*: arXiv:2402.13379 / NeurIPS 2024 Workshop
   - *URL*: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13379`
   - *Relevance*: Evaluates multi-turn tool safety risks and step-level defense guardrails.

2. **SODA Benchmark (2024)**:
   - *Title*: *The Cold-Start Safety Gap in LLM Agents*
   - *Authors*: Authors of SODA
   - *Venue*: arXiv:2406.07867
   - *URL*: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07867`
   - *Relevance*: Establishes that agent safety degrades as interaction history depth increases.

3. **ToolSafe (2024)**:
   - *Title*: *ToolSafe: Enhancing Tool Invocation Safety of LLM-based Agents via Proactive Step-level Guardrail and Feedback*
   - *Venue*: arXiv:2401.10156
   - *URL*: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10156`
   - *Relevance*: Step-level safety detection using interaction history feedback.

4. **API-Bank (2023)**:
   - *Title*: *API-Bank: A Benchmark for Tool-Augmented LLMs*
   - *Venue*: EMNLP 2023 (ACL Anthology: `2023.emnlp-main.187`)
   - *URL*: `https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.187/`
   - *Relevance*: Baseline tool-use capabilities benchmark.

5. **PALADIN (2024)**:
   - *Title*: *PALADIN: Empowering LLM Agents with Execution-Time Error Recovery*
   - *Venue*: arXiv:2402.14389
   - *URL*: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14389`
   - *Relevance*: Evaluates active error-recovery retrieval during tool execution.

6. **AgentAuditor (2025)**:
   - *Title*: *AgentAuditor: Human-Level Safety and Security Evaluations for LLM Agents*
   - *Venue*: OpenReview 2025 (`AgentAuditor2025`)
   - *URL*: `https://openreview.net/forum?id=AgentAuditor2025`
   - *Relevance*: Safety evaluation of autonomous agents.

7. **R-Judge (2024/2025)**:
   - *Title*: *R-Judge: Benchmarking Safety Risk Awareness for LLM Agents*
   - *Venue*: NeurIPS 2024 / ICLR 2025 (`RJudge2024`)
   - *URL*: `https://openreview.net/forum?id=RJudge2024`
   - *Relevance*: Benchmarking risk judgment in tool use.
