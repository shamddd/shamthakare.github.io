## 1. Primary References (Verified)

1. **Algorithms with Predictions (Mitzenmacher & Vassilvitskii, CACM 2022)**:
   - *Title*: *Algorithms with Predictions*
   - *Authors*: Michael Mitzenmacher, Sergei Vassilvitskii
   - *Venue*: Communications of the ACM (CACM), Vol. 65, No. 7, pp. 33–35, July 2022
   - *DOI*: `10.1145/3528087`
   - *Relevance*: Foundational framework for consistency vs robustness trade-offs in learning-augmented algorithms.

2. **SageDB: A Learned Database System (Kraska et al., SIGMOD 2019 / MLSys 2021)**:
   - *Title*: *SageDB: A Learned Database System*
   - *Authors*: Tim Kraska, Mohammad Alizadeh, Alex Beutel, Ed H. Chi, Jialin Ding, Ani Kristo, Guillaume Lample, Noah Pintar
   - *Venue*: SIGMOD 2019 / MLSys 2021
   - *URL*: `https://mlsys.org/Conferences/2021/accepted-papers`
   - *Relevance*: Demonstrates learned controllers in storage engines; highlights brittleness under distribution shift.

3. **Flexible Paxos (Howard et al., EuroSys 2016)**:
   - *Title*: *Flexible Paxos: Quorum Intersections Revisited*
   - *Authors*: Heidi Howard, Dahlia Malkhi, Alexander Spiegelman
   - *Venue*: EuroSys 2016 / arXiv:1608.06696
   - *DOI*: `10.1145/2901318.2901338`
   - *Relevance*: Demonstrates phase-dependent quorum sizing in consensus protocols.

4. **In Search of an Understandable Consensus Algorithm (Ongaro & Ousterhout, USENIX ATC 2014)**:
   - *Title*: *In Search of an Understandable Consensus Algorithm (Raft)*
   - *Authors*: Diego Ongaro, John Ousterhout
   - *Venue*: USENIX Annual Technical Conference (ATC), 2014
   - *URL*: `https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc14/technical-sessions/presentation/ongaro`
   - *Relevance*: Structural foundation for Raft joint-consensus state transitions and safety invariants.
