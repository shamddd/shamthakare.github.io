I am an independent computer science researcher and machine learning specialist studying reliable reasoning and adaptive computation for foundation models. My research investigates how learning and reasoning systems can make verifiable decisions when confidence signals, reasoning trajectories, internal states, and compute budgets are imperfect or changing. Currently, my primary work focuses on de-confounding token predictive entropy in reinforcement learning credit assignment (RLVR) and state-matched error recovery evaluation.

Professionally, I engineer high-throughput data processing pipelines and machine learning infrastructure at Spectra Corporate Service in Pune, India.

Research Focus Areas

  • Reliable Reasoning & Credit Assignment: Falsifying proxy uncertainty metrics, de-confounding predictive entropy in RLVR, and stabilizing policy gradient allocation across variable-length derivations.
  • State-Matched Recovery Evaluation: Formulating prospective structural covariate controls to evaluate neural reasoning error recovery under step-level verifier perturbations.
  • Adaptive Compute & Trade-offs: Characterizing deployment-amortized cost frontiers trading off pre-training compute volume against inference-time search search volume.
  • Learning-Augmented Systems: Designing uncertainty-calibrated trust gates for failure-domain aware Raft consensus adaptation and zero-knowledge enclave memory attestation.
