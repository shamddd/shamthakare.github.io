Strongest Empirical Findings

Tool-Agent Benchmark Blindness

In official ToolSandbox creation tasks under post-commit ACK loss (F1), blind retry produced duplicate persistent entities in 5/5 (100%) scenarios while the benchmark evaluator awarded similarity = 1.0. Milestone predicates tested existential presence without enforcing cardinality upper bounds.

Sufficiency of Standard Systems Baselines

Under clean observability, classical verify-before-retry (B3) achieved 100% VRS (10/10) and 0% false recovery with single-probe overhead in the experimental cost model. Idempotency-aware abstention (B2) secured safety in tested conditions but yielded 0% recovery coverage.

Model Post-Training Recovery Contrast

In StateShift, controlled endpoint recovery difference-in-differences interaction contrast reached Γ256 = +0.1176 (95% CI [+0.0955, +0.1400]) across checkpoint sequences, tracking recovery capacity gains during post-training.
