### PROJECT A — FLAGSHIP: *C3A: Causal Counterfactual Credit Assignment for Multi-Turn Tool-Using Foundation Agents*

- **Research Question**: In multi-turn agent interactions ($T \ge 20$) with external environments, can an unsupervised counterfactual state ablation estimator isolate the true marginal contribution of intermediate tool invocations from stochastic environment noise, outperforming trajectory-uniform advantage weighting without requiring external learned PRMs?

  - **Kianté Brantley** (SEAS / Kempner): Scientifically intersects with Brantley's work on *A\*PO* (2025/2026) and *REBEL* (2024), specifically addressing how non-gradient regression estimators and turn-level advantage decomposition can eliminate high policy gradient variance in sequential decision-making.
  - **Sham Kakade** (SEAS / Kempner): Scientifically intersects with Kakade's research on sample-efficient reward maximization and advantage estimation geometry in post-training.
  - **Finale Doshi-Velez** (SEAS): Scientifically intersects with Doshi-Velez's work on causal machine learning and off-policy credit evaluation under hidden latent confounding.
- **Stanford Faculty & Specific Scientific Intersect**:
  - **Chelsea Finn & Dorsa Sadigh** (CS / AI Lab): Scientifically intersects with Finn and Sadigh's *SPIRAL* (2026) framework and research on post-training flywheels for long-horizon agentic tool-use, specifically regarding how credit assignment over parallel versus sequential traces accelerates tool mastery.
  - **Emma Brunskill** (CS): Scientifically intersects with Brunskill's foundational work on data-efficient exploration, sample-efficient offline RL, and counterfactual policy evaluation in interactive domains.
  - **Percy Liang** (CRFM / HAI): Scientifically intersects with HELM-style evaluation of long-horizon coding and tool-use agents.
- **MIT Faculty & Specific Scientific Intersect**:
  - **Pulkit Agrawal** (EECS / CSAIL Improbable AI Lab): Scientifically intersects with Agrawal's research on interactive agent exploration, sensorimotor policy optimization, and credit assignment in stochastic multi-turn environments.

  - **Yoon Kim** (EECS / CSAIL): Scientifically intersects with Kim's work on efficient policy optimization and distributional RL.
